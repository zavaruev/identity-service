What to say in front of the camera

Recording instructions

Stand 0.7–1 m from the camera and speak in a normal voice — shouting makes this microphone clip. Keep the room quiet: turn off the TV and music. Choose the room, press “Record and add voice”, set 10–15 seconds and say 3–4 different phrases: “Computer, turn on the light in the living room.”, “Dobbi, turn off the light.”, “What is the weather like?”, “What time is it?”.

Different phrases and a natural tone matter more than repetition. One recording replaces the voice sample completely. Several WAV files can be uploaded at once through “Upload voice” — they are averaged.
